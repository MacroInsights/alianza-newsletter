Welcome to La Alianza

Edición Inaugural · 2026

Dear members, friends, and allies —

It is a true honor to welcome you to La Alianza's first newsletter. La Alianza was born from the conviction that when Latino professionals come together, share experiences, and work toward a common purpose, we can create an impact that goes beyond our careers and businesses. We build opportunities, strengthen our families, and contribute to the growth of our entire community.

Our mission, as La Alianza, is to drive professional development, leadership, business growth, and educational opportunity for Latino professionals across the Triad — by creating spaces to build relationships, share knowledge, develop new skills, and support those who come after us.

This newsletter will be an extension of that mission: news, events, resources, success stories, and opportunities to learn, collaborate, and grow together. We also believe firmly in opening doors for future generations — many of us got here because someone believed in our potential, and today we have the responsibility and privilege to do the same for others.

I invite you to take an active part in La Alianza — attend our events, share your experiences, and become part of a network that promotes collaboration, leadership, and collective success. Thank you for trusting this vision. This is just the beginning, and I'm convinced the best is yet to come.

With appreciation,
José A. Sandoval
President, La Alianza

Connecting leaders. Driving opportunity. Strengthening our community.

Quiénes Somos · Who We Are

Meet Our Leadership

“ If you want to go fast, go alone. If you want to go far, go together. ”

Si quieres ir rápido, ve solo. Si quieres llegar lejos, ve acompañado.

Behind La Alianza is a team of professionals who believe in the value of bringing people together. Our leadership represents a range of industries, backgrounds, and experiences, with a shared commitment to creating more opportunities for connection and professional growth across the Triad. As we begin this next chapter, we invite you to get to know the people helping shape LATNC and the work ahead.
